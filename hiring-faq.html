A different kind of FAQ

Questions people
don't usually ask.

Not a policy document. Not a corporate script. Just honest answers about what it can feel like to work here.

01

WORK

What does a typical day look like?

There isn't one.

Some days involve writing code. Some involve architecture discussions. Some involve debugging. Some involve talking to clients and understanding business problems.

We care much more about solving problems than following a rigid routine.

Do you only care about coding?

No.

Writing code is only one part of engineering. Understanding people, asking good questions, communicating clearly, and thinking critically are just as important.

02

PEOPLE

♥

This is the heart of the page.
Software doesn't build itself. People build it.

Do people actually matter here?

Yes.

The quality of our work is directly connected to the well-being, growth, curiosity, and happiness of the people creating it.

We care deeply about the systems we build. We care just as deeply about the people building them.

What if I don't know everything?

That's okay. Nobody does.

We're much more interested in people who are curious than people who pretend to know all the answers. Questions are welcome here.

Is it okay to make mistakes?

Absolutely.

Mistakes are part of engineering. The important part is learning, improving, and sharing what you've learned.

03

GROWTH

Will I receive mentorship?

Yes.

Learning doesn't stop after you're hired. Good engineers become great engineers by building, reviewing, asking questions, and working alongside other people.

Are internships treated differently?

Only in terms of experience.

Interns aren't there to watch from the sidelines. They're there to contribute, learn, ask questions, and grow.

Do you hire junior engineers?

Yes.

Potential matters. Curiosity matters. A willingness to learn matters. Experience is important, but it's only one part of the picture.

04

INTERVIEWS

◎This isn't meant to feel like an exam.
Introduction↓Conversation↓Technical discussion↓Meet the team↓Decision
Should I be nervous?

Probably. That's normal.

But remember: we're not trying to catch you making mistakes. We're trying to learn who you are, how you think, and whether we'd enjoy building things together.

What happens after I apply?

Usually: introduction → conversation → technical discussion → meeting the team → decision. We may adapt the process depending on the role.

05

INTERNSHIPS

I don't have a portfolio. Should I still apply?

Yes.

Show us what interests you. Tell us what you're learning. Share a personal project. Talk about a problem you solved. Perfection isn't required.

I changed careers. Can I still apply?

Absolutely.

Different experiences bring different perspectives.

06

REMOTE WORK

Can I work remotely?

Our working arrangements depend on the role and what the team needs. Where a role supports hybrid or remote work, we'll say so clearly on the role page.

How do remote teams stay connected?

Through deliberate communication: clear written updates, thoughtful meetings, code reviews, documentation, and plenty of room for questions.
